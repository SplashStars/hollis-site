Hollis keeps your life admin on your phone. By default nothing you type into Hollis leaves the device — there is no account to make, no server holding your bills, and no analytics watching you use the app.

Everything below explains the exceptions: the few features that do send something somewhere, what they send, and how to turn them off.

What Hollis stores on your phone

Your tasks, reminders, alarms, events, bills, subscriptions, warranties, documents and the things you own are written to a database on the device. That database is encrypted, and the key is held in the Android keystore, so another app cannot read it and it is not readable if the phone's storage is copied.

The Insights screen is calculated on the phone from the entries you already made. No figures are uploaded to produce it.

Photos and PDFs you add to the document vault stay on the phone. Text is read out of them by on-device recognition — the image is never sent anywhere to be read.

What leaves the phone, and only if you ask

1. Cloud backup and sync off until you sign in

If you create a Hollis account, your entries are copied to our cloud database so a new phone can restore them. The copy is stored on Supabase infrastructure. It contains what you typed into Hollis and your email address. Signing in uses a one-time code emailed to you — Hollis has no password to store. Until you turn backup on, nothing you typed is uploaded.

2. Ask Hollis off until you use it

When you ask Hollis a question, your question and the entries relevant to answering it are sent to our server, which passes them to an AI provider (Google Gemini and/or Anthropic) to compose the answer. The answer comes back and is shown to you. These providers process the request to produce the reply and do not use it to train their models. If you never open Ask Hollis, nothing is ever sent.

Your question and its answer are not stored on our server. What is stored is a count: how many AI requests your installation has made this month, so the fair-use limit can be enforced. The first time you use Ask Hollis without an account, Hollis asks our server for an anonymous identifier — a random ID tied to nothing but that count. It is not your name, your email or any device identifier, and it is deleted with your data.

3. Voice capture only while you hold the button

If you speak to Hollis instead of typing, Android's own speech recognition turns your words into text. Depending on your phone and its settings, that may be done by Google on Google's servers under Google's privacy policy. Hollis receives only the resulting text. Typing instead avoids this entirely.

4. Advertising only after the free period, and only on the free plan

Hollis is free for the first three months with no ads at all. After that you either subscribe, or keep using Hollis free with banner ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID to choose them. You can reset or delete that ID in Android Settings, and subscribing removes ads completely.

5. Payments

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Hollis never sees or stores your card details — we are told only whether a subscription is active.

What Hollis never does

  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling of your entries, ever, to anyone.
  • No sharing of them either, with one exception, described above: when you use Ask Hollis, what is needed to answer goes to the AI provider that composes the reply.
  • No reading your contacts, call log, SMS, location or other apps' data.
  • No advertising profile built from what you wrote in Hollis.

How long things are kept

On the phone, until you delete them or uninstall the app. In the cloud, until you delete your account — at which point the backup copy, your email address, your identifier and your usage count are all deleted. See Delete your account.

Your choices

  • Export everything. Settings & privacy → Your data → Export all my data. A file you keep.
  • Delete your cloud copy. Settings & privacy → Account & cloud backup → Delete account, or use the web form.
  • Use Hollis with no account at all. This is the default and always will be.

Children

Hollis is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your phone, the app will tell you in-app before the change takes effect.
