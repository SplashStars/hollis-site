Delete your Hollis account and data

Two ways to do it. Both delete the same thing.

HomePrivacyDelete your account

In the app — takes about ten seconds

  1. Open Hollis.
  2. Go to More → Settings & privacy.
  3. Tap Account & cloud backup, then Delete account.
  4. Confirm. The cloud copy is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Hollis offers to export your data first, so deleting the cloud copy does not have to mean losing it.

By email — if you no longer have the app installed

Send a message to esellersstores@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in, with "Delete my Hollis account" as the subject. We reply to confirm, then delete within 7 days.

We ask you to write from the signed-up address because that is how we know the request is yours. If you cannot, say so and we will find another way to verify it.

What gets deleted

Deleted straight awayKept, and for how long
Your account and email address.
Every entry synced to the cloud — tasks, reminders, alarms, events, bills, subscriptions, warranties, items.
Any documents you synced.
Any Ask Hollis conversation history held server-side. 		Nothing of your content.

Google Play keeps your purchase record independently of us, because it is a payment record — we cannot delete that, and you can see it in your Play account. Cancel a subscription in Google Play, not here.

What is on your phone stays on your phone. Deleting the account removes the cloud copy. To remove the on-device copy too, uninstall Hollis, or use Settings & privacy → Your data → Delete everything.

If you only want to stop paying

That is a different thing and does not need this page. Cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store → Subscriptions. Hollis keeps working, free, with ads. Your data is untouched.
