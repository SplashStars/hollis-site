Hollis

Never miss what matters.

Hollis is a personal life operating system for Android: the reminders, bills, subscriptions, warranties, appointments and documents that quietly run your life, in one app that works offline and keeps your data on your phone.

Reminders are treated as infrastructure, not a feature — an alarm rings until you answer it, a missed reminder is surfaced rather than swallowed, and nothing is ever silently marked done on your behalf.
